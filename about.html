Who we are

Specialists, not general hauliers.

Vector Specialist was built to handle the jobs ordinary couriers and removals firms can't. We focus on high-value, fragile and technical goods that need proper planning, the right equipment and people who know how to handle them.

From transport and handling through to on-site installation and commissioning, everything is delivered by one accountable team. That means fewer handovers, less risk, and a single point of contact who owns your project from start to finish.
